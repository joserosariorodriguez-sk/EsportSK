About the program

Built for players who want more than a game.

Simon Kenton Esports is designed to help students develop as competitors, teammates, and leaders. The program emphasizes disciplined practice, strategic thinking, and positive team culture while building a strong identity for our school community.

Our goal is not just to win matches, but to create a meaningful environment where students can grow, succeed, and represent Simon Kenton with professionalism and pride.

What we value

  • Respectful competition
  • Academic accountability
  • Team-first communication
  • Continuous improvement

Program standards

Expectations for every athlete.

  • Maintain strong academic standing and eligibility requirements
  • Arrive prepared and ready to contribute to team objectives
  • Demonstrate sportsmanship in every match and practice
  • Communicate respectfully with coaches, teammates, and opponents
  • Take ownership of individual development and team success
