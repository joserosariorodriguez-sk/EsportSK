01 // Player briefing

This is more than casual gaming.

Simon Kenton Esports is built around skill development, accountability, and healthy competition. We create a space where students can grow as players, teammates, and leaders while representing our school with pride.

Whether you are a returning competitor or just beginning to learn the game, our program encourages discipline, teamwork, and consistent effort.

Program goals

  • Develop strong team communication
  • Build competitive experience in real match settings
  • Support academic responsibility and school pride
  • Provide an inclusive environment for all skill levels
