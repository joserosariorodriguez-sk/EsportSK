Eligibility

Ready to compete?

Students interested in joining the team should be prepared to meet the academic, behavioral, and attendance expectations associated with school-sponsored competition.

Participation requires focus, accountability, and the ability to balance practice, schoolwork, and team responsibilities.

Basic requirements

  • Maintain passing grades
  • Follow school behavior expectations
  • Attend practices and team meetings
  • Demonstrate respectful communication

Participation standards

What we expect from athletes.

  • Be committed to team goals and game preparation
  • Prioritize school responsibilities while competing
  • Be coachable and willing to improve through feedback
  • Represent the program with professionalism on and off the screen
  • Communicate clearly and respectfully with teammates and staff
